the wand — write in the air, type into anything

2025 → now · co-founder · edge ml · nRF54LM20B

Co-founded Input-A (Inputa Inc., Delaware) to build the wand: a handheld that types what you write in the air. A compact neural net reads handwriting from a few-dollar motion sensor — recognition runs on the device in 25–28 ms on Nordic's nRF54LM20B, no camera, no microphone, no cloud — and it connects to TVs, phones and computers as a standard Bluetooth keyboard. Twenty years of small-silicon audio ML, pointed at motion instead of sound. Measured the honest way: 400+ paid study participants across 23 studies, scored on writers the model has never seen. Five provisional patent families filed. TVs today; the glasses era is the point.

racoon_alert — who’s raiding the pond

2026 · python · onvif · megadetector · speciesnet

I live in Berlin, and raccoons were raiding my pond — bothering the sticklebacks and the bitterling. I wanted to see how they were breaking in.

It turned into a garden camera system with no vendor cloud anywhere in it, and reverse-engineering the cheap Wi-Fi camera is what settled that: its pan/tilt runs through the manufacturer’s servers in plaintext, so that part is written up and parked rather than shipped, and everything useful comes over RTSP and ONVIF instead — the open standard that lets you talk to these cameras without their app. Video goes into a single Go media server on the LAN and plays sub-second in a browser; the target is that box standing alone on a Raspberry Pi with an HDMI screen and no route out. Nights are the hard part: MegaDetector finds the animal in the infrared frame and SpeciesNet — 65 million camera-trap images — names the species and is allowed to overrule it, which is why a black cat in the bushes is no longer e-mailed home as person 0.74. A stone bench had already fired 56 false person alerts in one day, so a detection now has to out-move the wobble that spot shows while nothing is happening there. The useful thing learned: viewing is cheap and detecting is not — MegaDetector at 1280 px is about five times a whole Pi 5 for one camera at 3 fps, so the standalone box needs an NPU.

ARP8 — acoustic arpeggiator

2026 · web midi · javascript

A browser MIDI arpeggiator for Korg Berlin's phase8 — the eight-voice acoustic synthesizer with steel resonators. Twenty-eight hand-authored generative compositions (Bach, Reich, Eno, Pärt, Cage, Satie…), each a different musical territory using one engine. Runs in Chrome over Web MIDI — no installer, no DAW.
